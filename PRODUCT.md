# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students, researchers, and interview reviewers evaluating visual speech
recognition from short uploaded or webcam-recorded clips. They need to move
quickly from video input to a legible transcript, timing evidence, and a
naturalized speech preview without learning the underlying model stack.

## Product Purpose

USR 2.0 Visual Speech Studio turns silent lip movement into English text and
then reconstructs plausible speech while preserving detected pauses. Success
means the input-to-result workflow is obvious, model state is trustworthy, and
technical detail remains available without competing with the primary task.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and credible. The product should feel like a well-made research
instrument prepared for a live demonstration, not a marketing page or an
experimental playground.

## Anti-references

Avoid an unbalanced two-column form with empty space on one side, nested cards,
decorative AI gradients, glassmorphism, oversized status copy, and controls
whose relationship to recognition versus speech synthesis is ambiguous.

## Design Principles

1. Follow the operator's sequence: connect, provide video, recognize, inspect,
   then listen.
2. Make the result—not configuration—the visual center of gravity.
3. Keep system confidence visible but quiet; expose implementation detail only
   on demand.
4. Preserve evidence: transcript, mouth crop, pause timeline, and audio should
   remain easy to compare.
5. Treat credentials as ephemeral request data and never persist or echo them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum action targets,
clear text labels in addition to icons, responsive single-column behavior, and
reduced-motion support. Errors must remain understandable without relying on
color alone.
